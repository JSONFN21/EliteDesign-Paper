\begin{abstract}
    \normalsize
    The aim of RNA design is to find, within an exponentially large search space, a nucleotide sequence 
    that folds into a given target structure. Efficient RNA design could aid in the development of RNA-based 
    therapeutics such as riboswitches and ribozymes. However, efficiently finding sequences with a high target 
    folding probability remains challenging, particularly for long RNAs. To address this challenge, we introduce 
    EliteDesign, an approach that uses the cross-entropy method to iteratively refine a distribution over valid 
    RNA sequences. Our method outperforms the state-of-the-art RNA design method, SamplingDesign, on Eterna100, achieving a better 
    average target folding probability while being 500 times faster. EliteDesign also scales to much longer RNA structures.
    On ten 16S rRNA targets ranging from 950 to 1,525 nucleotides, it achieves an average target folding probability of 0.51 
    versus 0.20 for SAMFEO while reducing the average runtime per puzzle by a factor of 24.
\end{abstract}
