The goal of RNA design is to find, within an exponentially large sequence
space, a nucleotide sequence that folds into a specified target structure.
While essential for developing functional RNAs such as riboswitches and ribozymes,
efficiently identifying sequences with high Boltzmann probability remains challenging,
especially for long sequences. Currently, the state-of-the-art method, SamplingDesign, is notoriously slow,
because its gradient-based optimization takes many small steps and scores thousands of samples at each step.
To address this bottleneck, we present EliteDesign, a novel and simple framework that leverages the
{\em gradient-free} cross-entropy method to iteratively refine a distribution over candidate RNA sequences.
On the Eterna100 benchmark, EliteDesign achieves a higher average Boltzmann probability than
SamplingDesign while running 178$\times$ faster. Furthermore, EliteDesign scales efficiently to long RNA structures
far beyond the reach of SamplingDesign. On \sixteenS{}, a set of ten 16S rRNA MFE structures (950--1,525 nucleotides),
it achieves an average Boltzmann probability of 0.52 versus 0.19 for
SAMFEO, while accelerating runtime by 14$\times$.
Our code is available at \url{https://github.com/JSONFN21/EliteDesign}.
